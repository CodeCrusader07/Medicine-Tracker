# 💊 Medicine Tracker

A simple and user-friendly Android application designed to help users manage their daily medicines, track medication schedules, and never forget their doses.

## 📱 About the App

Medicine Tracker helps users maintain a consistent medicine routine with reminders, daily checklists, and medication history.

## ✨ Features

- 💊 **Add Medicines** – Easily add medicines to your personal list.
- ⏰ **Medicine Reminders** – Get notifications at scheduled medicine times.
- ✅ **Daily Checklist** – Mark medicines as Taken or Missed.
- 🌅 **Daily Schedule** – Organize medicines into Morning, Afternoon, and Night.
- 📅 **Previous Days History** – View your previous medicine records.
- ❌ **Delete Medicines** – Remove medicines you no longer need.

## 🛠️ Built With

- Kotlin
- Android Studio
- Jetpack Compose
- Material Design 3
- Android Notifications

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Download the Medicine Tracker APK.
2. Install it on your Android device.
3. Open the application.
4. Add your medicines and reminder times.
5. Mark each medicine as Taken, Missed or Delete.

## 🎯 Purpose

The main purpose of Medicine Tracker is to make daily medication management easier, more organized, and convenient.

## 🔒 Privacy

Medicine records are stored locally on the device in the current app version. Keep your device secure and maintain your own backup if needed.

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

This application is a personal medication tracking tool. It does not provide medical advice or replace prescriptions or guidance from healthcare professionals.

Always follow your doctor's prescribed medication schedule.
